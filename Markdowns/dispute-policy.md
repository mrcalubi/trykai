# Dispute Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

## What TryKai is

TryKai is a marketplace that connects independent hosts with guests. We are not the host, and hosts are not TryKai employees or contractors. When something goes wrong, it is primarily a matter between the guest and the host involved. That said, we do not disappear once a booking is made. This page explains how we help, and what you can expect from us.

There are three kinds of situations this policy covers: complaints about the quality of a session, safety concerns, and appeals against a host strike. Each is handled differently, outlined below.

## Quality complaints

If a session did not match what was described, or something about the experience fell short (for example, the host was very late, or the session was cut short), you can raise this with us within 7 days of the session.

To raise a complaint, email us at trykaisg@gmail.com with your booking reference, a description of what happened, and any evidence that helps us understand the situation, such as photos or messages. Including evidence upfront generally helps us resolve things faster.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Once we have reviewed the situation, possible outcomes include a partial or full refund, a warning to the host, a strike against the host's account, or no action if we find the complaint is not supported.

## Safety reports

Safety reports are treated differently from quality complaints, and there is no time limit on raising one. If you ever feel unsafe during or after a session, please tell us, regardless of how much time has passed.

If you are in immediate danger, please contact the police first, then let us know what happened once you are safe.

When we receive a credible safety report, we may suspend the account in question immediately while we review the situation, rather than waiting for a pattern of behaviour to emerge. Severe safety violations can result in permanent removal from the platform, independent of the standard strike system used for cancellations.

TryKai may cooperate with the police or other relevant authorities if requested, and may report serious incidents proactively where appropriate.

## Appealing a host strike

If a host cancellation or no show was caused by something genuinely outside the host's control, the host can appeal within 7 days of the cancellation, with a short explanation of what happened. We review appeals manually, and if successful, the strike is removed from the host's account. Appeals only affect whether the host keeps a strike. They never affect the guest's refund, which stays the same either way. This mirrors what is described in our Cancellation Policy.

## If a payout has already been released

Host payouts are normally released 24 hours after a session starts. In rare cases, a dispute may be confirmed after that payout has already gone out, for example if a safety report or complaint comes in a few days later. When this happens, TryKai will refund the guest first, treat the amount as a cost to us in the meantime, and work separately to recover it from the host, including from future payouts where applicable. The host issue itself, such as a strike or suspension, is handled independently of this recovery process.

## What we can and cannot do

We can review evidence, communicate with both parties, apply refunds in line with our Cancellation and Refund policies, issue strikes, suspend or permanently remove accounts, and hold a payout while a dispute is under review.

We are not a court or an arbitrator, and we cannot guarantee a specific outcome. Our full liability terms are set out in our Terms of Service. For anything involving physical harm, property damage, or a safety incident, we also encourage you to contact the relevant authorities directly, this is not something we can resolve on your behalf.

## Reviews and disputes

Reviews are the main long term accountability mechanism on TryKai. We generally will not remove a review just because one party disagrees with it, unless it violates our content guidelines, such as harassment, false claims, or sharing someone's personal information. If you believe a review is factually inaccurate in a material way, contact us with details.
