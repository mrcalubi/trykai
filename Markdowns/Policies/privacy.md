# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how TryKai collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

## 1. Who we are

TryKai (UEN 53526159D) is a sole proprietorship registered in Singapore, operating trykai.sg.

**Data Protection Officer:** Caleb Ong, privacy@trykai.sg

Contact our Data Protection Officer for any question about your data or to exercise your rights below.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, display name, email, password | Signup | To create and run your account |
| Profile photo | If you add one | Shown on your profile, listings and reviews |
| Listing details, photos, general area, full address | When you create a listing | To show your listing. Your full address is only shared with guests who have a confirmed booking |
| Booking and payment records | When you book or are booked | To process bookings, refunds and payouts |
| Reviews and review photos | When you leave a review | A Guest's review of a Host (including photos) is shown publicly on the Host's listing and profile once it is live. A Host's written review of a Guest is shown publicly on the Guest's profile once it is live. The Host's star rating of a Guest is not shown publicly; it only counts toward that Guest's own milestones. An optional private note on a review is seen only by TryKai |
| Identity verification result | Before you host | To confirm Hosts are real, identifiable people |
| Your name, date of birth and mobile number as held by Singpass, and a Singpass identifier for your TryKai account | When you verify to host with Singpass | To confirm Hosts are real, identifiable adults, that one person has one host account, and to reach Hosts about their sessions |
| ID document and selfie | When you verify to host by upload | Same as above |
| Your choice about offer emails | Signup, or later in Settings | To email you new sessions and offers, at most once a week, only if you agreed. Every such email has a one-tap unsubscribe link, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time. Booking emails and reminders are not affected |
| Basic usage data (pages viewed, device type) | Automatically | To keep the site working and improve it |

We do not collect your full card details. Stripe handles them.

## 3. What is public

Your display name, profile photo, ID verified badge, listings, the reviews you receive as a Host (and any photos attached to those reviews), and the written reviews Hosts leave about you as a Guest are visible to anyone once those reviews are live. Star ratings Hosts give Guests are not public; they only count toward your own milestones. Private notes on reviews are seen only by TryKai. Your full name, email, phone number and bookings are not public. If you take a seat on someone else's booking, the person who booked and the Host can see your display name, and you can see the address, arrival directions and day-of contact number as that booking's guests can. A Host's full address is shown only to guests with a confirmed booking for that listing. If a Host turns on a day-of contact number, confirmed guests of that session can see it from 24 hours before until the session ends, only to find the Host or sort last-minute logistics.

## 4. Identity verification

Where it is offered, Hosts can verify with **Singpass**. With your consent, Singpass shares your name, date of birth and mobile number, and gives us an identifier for you that is specific to TryKai. You see these before submitting. We use your date of birth to confirm you are 18 or older, and your mobile number to reach you about your sessions. We do not receive your NRIC number or any document images.

Hosts can instead verify by uploading a photo of their passport or NRIC and a selfie. These are stored in private, access-restricted storage and reviewed only by the TryKai team. We use them only to confirm your identity. On an NRIC you may cover the number: we need only your name and photo.

- Images from a rejected attempt are deleted after 30 days.
- Images for an approved Host are kept while the account is active.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run TryKai:

- **Stripe:** payments and Host payouts
- **Singpass (GovTech):** identity verification for Hosts who choose it
- **Supabase:** our database, file storage and logins
- **Resend:** sending our emails
- **Vercel:** hosting the website
- **OneMap (Singapore Land Authority):** look up and verify listing addresses from a postal code

Some of these providers store data outside Singapore. We use providers who protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

We may also disclose personal data when required by law or a court order, or to protect someone's safety.

**We do not sell personal data.**

## 6. How long we keep it

- **While your account is active:** we keep your data as long as we need it to run your account.
- **After you close your account:** your profile data is deleted within 6 months.
- **Booking and payment records** are kept for 5 years after the transaction, for tax, accounting and dispute purposes.
- **Verification images:** see section 4.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the personal data we hold about you
- correct anything inaccurate
- withdraw consent for a particular use (this may stop you using parts of TryKai)
- close your account

Email privacy@trykai.sg. We aim to respond within 30 days.

## 8. Security

We use access-restricted databases, encrypted connections and private storage to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data breaches

If a breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and affected users as the PDPA requires.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will tell you about material changes by email or on the site.
